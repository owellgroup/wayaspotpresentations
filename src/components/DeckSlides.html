Multi-Tenant · Per Business
How It Works for Each Client

Every business gets its own profile. We set them up. They run the full platform.

Their credentials and profile are used across every feature in Wayaspot — not marketing only. Marketing, Meta & Google publishing, WhatsApp flows, sales, leave management, appointments, applications, and analytics all run inside that one isolated business account.

01

We set up their account

Wayaspot creates the business profile and workspace — company details, users, modules, and permissions — so every feature is ready under their credentials from day one.

02

They submit their credentials

The business provides Meta (Facebook / Instagram), Google, WhatsApp Business, and related channel details. Those credentials are bound to their profile and power every module they use.

03

They run all features themselves

Once live, they manage marketing, announcements, services, sales, WhatsApp flows, leave management, appointments, and other operations — all from their own dashboard.

04

Fully automated & fully scheduled

Marketing campaigns can be scheduled and sent automatically across their connected channels. No manual posting across apps — Wayaspot publishes on their schedule.

05

WhatsApp flows per business

Their profile runs its own WhatsApp flows — applications, registrations, follow-ups, and customer intake — using that company’s connected WhatsApp credentials.

06

Operations under the same profile

Leave management, doctor appointments, meeting rooms, and other ops sit in the same business profile — same credentials, separate data from every other client, managers see only their company.

Investor takeaway: one business profile + their credentials power the entire system — marketing, WhatsApp, sales, leave management, and operations — automated, scheduled, and fully isolated per client.
